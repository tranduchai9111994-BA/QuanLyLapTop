## kNN và khoảng cách

3. Beyer, K., Goldstein, J., Ramakrishnan, R., & Shaft, U. (1999). When is "nearest neighbor" meaningful? In *Proceedings of the 7th International Conference on Database Theory (ICDT)* (pp. 217–235). Springer.

5. Lops, P., de Gemmis, M., & Semeraro, G. (2011). Content-based recommender systems: State of the art and trends. In F. Ricci, L. Rokach, B. Shapira, & P. Kantor (Eds.), *Recommender Systems Handbook* (pp. 73–105). Springer.
